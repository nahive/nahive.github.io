花火 · Fireworks for iOS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Fireworks checks whether terrain blocks your view of a fireworks display or a landmark. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the developer does not receive or store any information about you.

What the app does not do

Fireworks does not ask you to sign in, does not include analytics or advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites, and does not save your points, searches or results. Nothing is sent to servers run by the developer (there are none).

Your location

Location access is optional and only used while the app is open. The app asks for it only when you choose “My Location” or the locate button. If you have already allowed it, the app starts your viewpoint at your current position. Your location is used on your device to place the viewpoint and center the map. It is never sent to the developer. Like any point you choose, it determines which map areas are requested from the services below.

You can turn location access off at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Fireworks. The app works fully without it: search for a place or press and hold the map instead.

Services the app contacts

To draw the map and compute a verdict, the app downloads data from these providers. Each request reveals the approximate area you are looking at and, as with any internet request, your IP address. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies.

ProviderPurposeWhat it receives
Apple (MapKit) Map display, place search, naming dropped pins Map area viewed, search text, pin coordinates. Apple Privacy Policy
Amazon Web Services — Terrain Tiles (AWS Open Data) Elevation along the line between your two points Requests for elevation map tiles covering that line. AWS Privacy Notice
OpenStreetMap Overpass API (overpass-api.de, with overpass.private.coffee as fallback) Building footprints, only when “Include buildings” is on and the points are within 40 km A rectangular area that contains the line. overpass-api.de, Private.coffee

iOS may temporarily cache downloaded map and elevation data on your device. You can clear it by deleting the app.

Crash reports

If you have enabled “Share With App Developers” in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash and diagnostic reports with the developer. These are controlled by Apple and your settings, not by the app.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
