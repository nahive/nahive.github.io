How it works

Set a viewpoint and a target (fireworks or a landmark). The app samples the terrain along the straight line between them. It accounts for the curvature of the Earth and atmospheric refraction, then checks whether any ground rises above the sight line. Fireworks mode adds the burst altitude to the target. Landmark mode adds the height of the landmark itself.

Common questions

How do I place a point? Search in the From/To fields, use “My Location”, choose “Choose on Map”, or press and hold the map. Drag a pin to move it.

It says visible, but I couldn't see anything. Results are estimates from terrain data (~30 m resolution). Trees, most structures, weather, haze and the exact launch position aren't included. Buildings from OpenStreetMap are included within 40 km when available, but coverage varies.

What does “No data” mean? Elevation couldn't be downloaded, usually because the connection dropped. Check your connection and retry.

Does it work offline? No. Elevation and map data are downloaded as you use the app.

Is my location shared? No. See the privacy policy.

Not for safety-critical use

Fireworks is for planning where to watch from. Don't use it for navigation, aviation, emergencies or anything safety-related.
